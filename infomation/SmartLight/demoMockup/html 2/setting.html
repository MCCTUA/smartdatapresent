Routing Tab

Alert, recovery, maintenance, and escalation policy. Parent account receives child alerts only by explicit opt-in.

Customer own alertsSend critical and warning alerts to selected customer recipients.
Dealer child-customer alertsDealer receives only selected severity/event/customer combinations.
Platform portfolio alertsPlatform receives system-wide or escalated alerts, not every customer alert by default.
Recovery messagesSend recovered/closed messages when the customer option is enabled.
Maintenance lifecycleNotify when maintenance is created, updated, completed, or breached.
